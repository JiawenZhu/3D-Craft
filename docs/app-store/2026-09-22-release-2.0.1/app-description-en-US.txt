Start with an idea. Bring it into 3D. Play it in games. Animate it into life.

3D Craft is the next-generation multi-model AI studio for creators, game developers, 3D printing enthusiasts, and concept artists. Transform simple text prompts or 2D photos into high-fidelity textured 3D models and looping character animation videos in seconds.

Whether crafting indie game assets, 3D printing custom miniatures, or directing animated character shorts, 3D Craft puts the world's top generative 3D, physics gaming, and video models at your fingertips.

— HOW IT WORKS
——————————
1. Describe or Upload: Enter a text prompt or upload a reference photo.
2. Select Your Model: Choose your preferred 3D or video engine.
3. Bring to Life: Generate textured 3D meshes or animate into video with one tap.
4. Play, Export & Build: Play in games, download standard GLB/OBJ files, or export to your game engine and 3D slicer.
