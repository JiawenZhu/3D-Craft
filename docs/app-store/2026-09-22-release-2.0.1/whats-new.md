# Version 2.0.1 — App Store Release Metadata & What's New

## 1. What's New in This Version (此版本的新增功能)

### English (U.S.) — en-US
```text
3D Craft 2.0.1 brings your creations to life in playable worlds and introduces powerful organizational tools:

• Play Your 3D Creations in Games: Your custom-created 3D models can now jump straight into interactive 3D physics game worlds! Experience your models in action with blazing-fast launch speeds and real-time loading progress.
• Favorites Collection: Long-press any 3D model, concept artwork, animation, or chat draft to save it to your dedicated Favorites tab for instant, permanent access.
• Smart Archive & Cleanup: Clean up your active workspace without losing your ideas. Archived creations, projects, and drafts are kept safely for 30 days with one-tap restore and permanent delete controls.
• Codex Game Studio for Animations: Turn looping character animations into playable games! Export structured prompts and game blueprints directly to Codex or AI coding agents for endless runners, 2D platformers, and virtual companion games.
• Desktop Emotional Companion: Set your favorite looping animations as interactive desktop widgets with dynamic character switching.
• Ultra-Fast Loading & Performance: Intelligent pre-warming and persistent asset caching deliver instant 3D viewport rendering and buttery-smooth navigation.
```
