Welcome to 3D Craft 1.0 (15)! Please test on your iPhone or iPad with an internet connection. Your creations and wallet sync with your 3D Craft account.

1. Sign in and account switching
Try Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Check the new welcome/sign-in screens, password visibility, and sign out/sign in. Each account should show only its own creations and balance.

2. Token purchases and Wallet
Test a Token pack or subscription using the TestFlight purchase confirmation. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox and do not charge real money. Purchases are available to every signed-in app account, not just a test email.
After a successful purchase, the app should open Wallet, show +Tokens, and animate the total once the purchase is verified. If syncing takes time, use Retry in Wallet; do not repeat the purchase just to refresh it. The sync message should clear when resolved and should not cover other screens. Switching accounts must not move another account's purchased Tokens.

3. Create an image and a 3D model
Enter an idea or add a reference image. Check the Token estimate before confirming. Test the AI-sharing permission: Not now should stop the request; Allow should continue. Generate a concept, select it, add 3D instructions, and choose an engine. Check progress, completion, the 3D viewer, lighting, and export. Close/reopen the app and verify your saved creation is still in Library.

4. Play and share games
Open Games and try the published games, including Moonrun, Moss Robot, and Emberwing. Check touch controls and landscape play where suggested. Like a game, tap again to undo, and check the count updates.
To share your own game: Games > + > Share a game. Paste a public playable HTTPS link from your published Claude/Codex game, enter its title and creator, check/open the link, confirm play-testing and rights, then submit. It should appear in My submissions as pending review before public publication. Conversation links and local computer addresses are not playable public game links.

5. Appearance and feedback
Try English/Chinese and the available appearances. Check for clipped text, keyboard overlap, and controls that are difficult to tap. Send TestFlight feedback with a screenshot, device model, and steps to reproduce. Please do not include passwords or payment details.
