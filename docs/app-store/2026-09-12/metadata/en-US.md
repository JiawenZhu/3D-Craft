## Description

Start with an idea. Bring it into 3D.

3D Craft is a creative studio for turning text and reference images into concept art and 3D assets. Explore expressive characters, playful animals, scenery, and objects, then develop an idea of your own.

CREATE THROUGH CONVERSATION
Describe what you imagine and refine your direction in a conversation. Add a reference image and instructions when you already know the look you want. Generated concepts stay in the same creative workflow.

BRING YOUR CONCEPT INTO 3D
Choose the concept you want to use as the source for your model. View the result from different angles, zoom into details, and explore material, solid, and wire views.

FIND YOUR LIGHT
Adjust lighting, environment fill, and exposure while previewing your model. Choose the emerald or lavender appearance that feels right for your studio.

KEEP YOUR IDEAS TOGETHER
Revisit your projects and creations in your library. Browse public examples for inspiration while keeping your own creations separate.

TAKE YOUR ASSET FURTHER
Export your GLB model, or share it with an editable browser-game brief for ChatGPT or Claude Code. The handoff includes your model and instructions; the external tool builds the game. Sharing does not automatically generate or publish a game.

Generation requires an internet connection and available generation credits. Paid credit packs and an optional auto-renewing subscription are planned for launch; final availability and localized prices must be shown in the app before purchase. External AI services may require a separate account and have their own limits. Generated results can vary and may need refinement. Only upload material you have permission to use.
