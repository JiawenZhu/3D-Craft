# Gallery-first creation — design exploration

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Two concept boards, not implemented app screens or measured 3D-rendering results. The uploaded Grok screenshot was inspected for the gallery and floating composer pattern; the generation calls used text descriptions, not the original image as an attached reference.

Color values were read from the current CraftAppearance.swift. Model pricing remains optional; a small calculator entry preserves the clean header. Suggested implementation motion: card selection lifts subtly; selected image remains the transition anchor into 3D; podium and object share one real 3D scene. Respect reduced motion. These are suggested interaction directions, not animations demonstrated by the still images.

Files: soft-lavender-v1.png and emerald-green-v1.png.

## Soft lavender — exact generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity product design presentation for our existing native iOS app named "3D Craft". This is an ORIGINAL app mockup, inspired by a mobile photo-creation app's immersive image grid and bottom floating prompt bar, not a copy of its logo or photos. Deliver a beautifully polished landscape 3-screen board, very high resolution, approximately 2400x1600, showing three complete tall iPhone screens side by side with narrow neutral device outlines, aligned at the same height, ample outer white margins. Large legible UI text. No tiny annotations, no clipped screens.
Our user journey is inspiration → create/select concept images → inspect an actual 3D object and optionally play with it. Showcase premium cute game assets: a ginger adventurer cat in teal embroidered jacket and charcoal trousers, a toy racing car, an island with coconut palms, a tiny forest cottage, a baby dragon and a stylized waterfall diorama. Assets must be beautiful detailed 3D-style artwork, not flat icons, diverse and expertly composed.
SCREEN 1: compact header cube mark + "3D Craft", right small outline cost-calculator icon and personal circular avatar. Heading "What will you create?" with modest size so imagery dominates. Filter pills "For you" selected, "Characters", "Objects", and a small sort icon. Two-column beautifully rounded image masonry gallery, six lush inviting artwork tiles at varied heights, light pastel backgrounds, subtle small pill labels "Character", "Vehicle", "World". Show cat large, car, dragon, cottage, palms, waterfall. A floating frosted white rounded input capsule is pinned above a compact bottom tab bar; attachment plus on left, placeholder "Describe or upload an idea", colored circular upward arrow on right. The bar has a real backdrop blur, restrained shadow and hairline. Separate adjacent small sliders button accesses model/style settings. Bottom tabs "Create" selected, "Library", "Profile". Do not put another search field above gallery. This bar invites creation, not just content search. Gallery content must have sufficient bottom inset to remain reachable.
SCREEN 2: back arrow, title "Concept studio". Compact progress rail "Idea — Concept — 3D" with Concept active. Heading "Find their personality." Short editable prompt capsule "A tiny forest adventurer". A large portrait concept card of our ginger cat, with peek edges of two alternative cards on each side; clear selected checkmark. Below, three small thumbnail alternative styles, caption "The little adventurer", a subtle "Refine" text action. Fixed bottom white action area, one full-width theme button "Generate 3D model". This selected image is clearly the source for the next screen. No floating mysterious wire overlays that pretend a finished model exists. Small secondary label "Selected concept".
SCREEN 3: back, centered "YOUR STUDIO", share and small cost-calculator icons. Rail "Concept — 3D — Play" with 3D active. Title "Lantern Cat", subtitle "Made by you". A large genuinely volumetric render of THE SAME ginger cat, same clothes, standing with feet touching a subtle white physical turntable pedestal, contact shadow, fully integrated lighting and perspective. Bright and visible on every side, pale atmospheric background. Three restrained round controls along right edge: rotate, lighting, expand. "Drag to explore" underneath. Small segmented "Material / Solid / Wire" with Material active. Bottom action "Try in a game" filled theme color, secondary "Export model". No auto-entering a game.
Use crisp SF-style typography with strong hierarchy, dark readable text, realistic iOS safe areas, generous layout, precise modern line icons, subtly glowing image tiles and beautiful material depth. Do not add visible API names, prices, token rates, loading spinners, big settings panels, overwhelming paragraphs, promotional Grok branding or magenta. Communicate liveliness through a lifted selected card, soft depth, gleaming materials and a coherent image-to-3D transformation, while all screens remain implementable UI.
Place small board labels below the devices: "01  Explore & imagine", "02  Choose your concept", "03  Bring it into 3D".
COLOR THEME: Soft lavender, matching our actual app. Warm white #FCFAFE dominates, restrained soft lavender #D8A1F1, lilac highlights #EEDBFA, deeper lavender #B074DE only in primary buttons and selections, text accents muted plum #68437B, darkest text #302238. No hot pink or fuchsia. Use a subtle soft lavender-to-white ambient glow. Presentation heading outside devices: "3D Craft · Soft lavender".
