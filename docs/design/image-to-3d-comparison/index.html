04 / Make your own choice

What matters for your creation?

There is no single model that fits every picture or budget. Use the pictures, 3D viewer, and price table together.

Look, price, and download size

Start with the result that looks right to you, then rotate it to see whether the back and sides meet your needs. Compare the price and original file size before choosing it in the app. The app shows its current Token price before a generation begins.

What this test cannot tell you

We tested one dragon once with each model. A person, prop, or another picture may turn out differently. Meshy’s “multi-image” result used only one picture, so it does not show what extra views could improve. The higher-priced HI3D examples are completed samples, and all listed models remain available in the app.
